PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Organic Cleaning Products E-Commerce Platform
Version 1.0
1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Product Name: TBD
Product Type:
A modern, natural, e-commerce website for an organization that develops and sells bioenzyme-based and organic cleaning products, while educating customers about environmental sustainability, organic waste reuse, and responsible cleaning.
Initial Product Catalog:
1. Bioenzyme
2. Dishwasher
3. Laundry Washing Liquid / Detergent Liquid
4. Shampoo
5. Floor Cleaner
The catalog must support additional products later without requiring architectural changes.
Product Sizes:
500 ml, 1 L, 2 L, 3 L, 4 L, 5 L, and custom quantity. Customers can directly increase quantity rather than being restricted to predefined package sizes.
2. PRODUCT VISION
Create a trustworthy digital storefront combining modern e-commerce, an organic/natural brand identity, environmental education, product transparency, online purchasing, organizational bulk purchasing, environmental activity storytelling, and educational content.
Core positioning:
Environmental responsibility + effective cleaning.
The website must not rely on exaggerated marketing, fabricated statistics, fake testimonials, or unsupported safety/environmental claims.
3. PRIMARY OBJECTIVES
Business objectives:
Sell products online.
Showcase the complete product catalog.
Generate customer enquiries.
Support Andhra Pradesh and Telangana initially.
Support household and organizational customers.
Support bulk purchases.
Build trust around the environmental mission.
Explain the production process.
Publish environmental and educational content.
Establish a professional digital presence.
Allow routine management without developer involvement.
Customer objectives:
Understand the organization and products.
Explore and compare products.
Search products and content.
Add products to cart.
Purchase multiple products together.
Purchase large quantities.
Apply discounts.
Save products to a wishlist.
Track orders.
Request cancellations and returns/refunds according to policy.
Read verified customer reviews.
Learn about the production process and environmental activities.
Contact the organization easily.
4. TARGET USERS
Household consumers, environment-conscious consumers, general consumers, and organizations such as hotels, restaurants, offices, schools, apartment communities, institutions, and other businesses.
5. GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE
Initial delivery region: Andhra Pradesh + Telangana.
Future expansion to other Indian states should be supported.
Business location: TBD.

52. SUCCESS CRITERIA
The website should:
1. Convert visitors into customers.
2. Explain the product concept clearly.
3. Build trust through authentic information.
4. Make purchasing simple.
5. Support household and organizational customers.
6. Educate visitors about environmental activities.
7. Establish a credible online brand.
8. Allow the organization to manage products, orders, and content.
9. Provide a foundation for expansion beyond Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
53. GUIDING PRINCIPLE
The website should never look like an AI-generated template or generic sustainability website.
It should feel like a real product company with a real environmental purpose.
Every major element should answer:
What is the product?
Why should I trust it?
How can I buy it?
The environmental story should strengthen the product experience rather than distract from it.
The e-commerce experience should remain simple, fast, authentic, and trustworthy.
