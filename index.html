The 90-Day Cycle

From Fruit Waste to Powerful Cleaner

No synthetic accelerants or heat treatments. We respect the natural 90-day anaerobic biological timetable to yield stable, high-potency enzymes.

01

Daily Peel Sourcing

Fresh orange and sweet lime (Mosambi) peels are collected daily within 6 hours of juicing from partner stalls in Hyderabad & Vijayawada.

02

3:1:10 Golden Ratio

3 parts washed peels, 1 part unrefined mineral-rich jaggery, and 10 parts clean water are blended in food-grade anaerobic fermenters.

03

90-Day Fermentation

Beneficial wild cultures ferment under controlled conditions, breaking down sugars and synthesizing active hydrolytic enzymes.

04

Micro-Filter & Test

Liquid is double filtered to remove pulp (which becomes rich garden compost). Every batch is pH-tested to ensure optimum acidity (3.5–4.0).

The Science of 90-Day Bioenzyme Fermentation

We believe customers have a right to understand the exact microbiology and chemistry behind what they use to clean their homes.

The 3:1:10 Foundation

The biological foundation of authentic bioenzymes relies on a strict ratio discovered by agricultural microbiologists: 3 parts fresh citrus peels, 1 part raw unrefined jaggery, and 10 parts dechlorinated water.

1. Fresh Peels (3 Parts)

Provides natural organic d-limonene, citric acid, and the wild microorganisms residing on citrus rinds.

2. Raw Jaggery (1 Part)

Unrefined jaggery serves as mineral-dense nutrition for probiotic yeasts and lactic bacteria.

3. Pure Water (10 Parts)

Dechlorinated deep borewell water tested for mineral equilibrium to enable active yeast growth.

What Happens Over 90 Days?

  1. Days 1–30: Anaerobic Glycolysis: Wild yeasts convert simple sugars into alcohol and carbon dioxide. Fermentation tanks are degassed daily to maintain ideal internal pressure.
  2. Days 31–60: Acetic & Lactic Conversion: Beneficial bacteria oxidize alcohol into organic acetic and lactic acids, dropping the solution pH below 4.0.
  3. Days 61–90: Enzymatic Maturation: Autolysis of yeast cells releases concentrated intracellular enzymes—predominantly Lipase (lipid cleaver), Protease (protein digester), and Amylase (carbohydrate disperser).

Zero Waste Byproduct Guarantee

When liquid bioenzyme is filtered out, the spent citrus fruit pulp is not discarded. It is mixed with dried neem leaves and dry carbon materials to produce nutrient-rich bio-compost, distributed directly to urban rooftop gardeners and regenerative farms in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Our Roots & Purpose

Rooted in Andhra Pradesh & Telangana

Why we started Prakriti Bio, our refusal to greenwash, and our mission to detoxify domestic greywater.

In 2023, while volunteering on lake restoration projects around the Musi and Krishna river basins, our founders observed a persistent tragedy: residential greywater was choked with linear alkylbenzene sulfonates, synthetic bleach, and artificial quaternary ammonium compounds from ordinary household cleaners.

Meanwhile, at fruit stalls on every street corner of Hyderabad and Vijayawada, massive heaps of fresh sweet lime and orange peels were being thrown into municipal garbage trucks every afternoon—destined to decompose into methane in overflowing landfills.

The question was simple: Can we close the loop? Can we transform this mountain of fragrant citrus peel waste into effective, probiotic cleaners that leave domestic drainage water completely safe for urban soils and waterways?

Prakriti Bio was born from that conviction. Today, we partner directly with local fruit vendors, paying fair collection incentives, and fermenting pure bioenzymes without a single synthetic dye, toxic foaming surfactant, or artificial fragrance.
